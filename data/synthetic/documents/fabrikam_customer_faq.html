Fabrikam Aerial Logistics — Frequently Asked Questions

What are your service hours?

Delivery operations run 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM local time, seven days a week. Medical deliveries on the Heron H1 run 24/7.

How long is the hardware warranty?

All drones include a 2-year limited hardware warranty. Batteries are covered for 12 months or 400 charge cycles, whichever comes first. An extended 3-year CarePlus warranty is available for $2,400 per drone.

What is the return policy?

Unused drones in original packaging may be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened units are subject to a 15% restocking fee.

Which regions do you operate in?

We currently operate in Washington, Oregon, Colorado, and Nova Scotia. Expansion to British Columbia is planned for 2027.

What happens if weather grounds my delivery?

Deliveries are automatically rescheduled to the next available window and the customer is notified by SMS and email. Weather-related delays are not eligible for service credits.

How do I reach support?

Email support@fabrikam-aerial.example or call 1-800-555-0142. Enterprise customers have a dedicated support engineer and a 1-hour response SLA for severity-1 issues.
